Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Our Privacy Commitment

At co-pon, we believe digital tools should respect your privacy. Our core philosophy is simple: zero bloat, zero tracking, and complete transparency. This Privacy Policy outlines how your data is handled when you use our digital coupon wallet.

1. Information We Collect

  • Google Account Data

    When you sign in using Google Sign-In, we collect basic account information such as your name, profile email address, and unique user identifier. This is solely used to authenticate your identity and secure your wallet.

  • Wallet Content

    We store the coupons, images, text, and categories you explicitly save inside your co-pon wallet so you can access them across your devices.

  • Partner Sharing Data

    If you subscribe to our premium service and choose to share your wallet, we process the Gmail address of the designated partner solely to grant them access to your shared wallet.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly to provide and maintain the core service:

  • To authenticate your account securely via Google Sign-In.
  • To synchronize your coupons and wallet settings across authorized devices.
  • To enable partner wallet sharing for premium subscribers.
  • To manage your premium subscription status.

3. Data Protection & What We NEVER Do

Your privacy and device performance are our highest priorities. We strictly adhere to the following principles:

  • No Third-Party Tracking: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to ad networks, data brokers, or third parties.
  • No Ads or Ad Trackers: co-pon is ad-free. We do not embed behavioral tracking scripts or third-party ad technology.
  • No Unnecessary Background Processing: We do not run hidden background tracking that causes battery drain.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your wallet data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption protocols. Authentication is handled directly through Google Sign-In, ensuring your password is never stored on our servers.

5. Managing & Deleting Your Data

You have full control over your information at all times:

  • Delete Coupons: Removing a coupon in the app permanently deletes it from your wallet.
  • Account Deletion: You can request full deletion of your account and all associated wallet data at any time through the app settings or by contacting support.
