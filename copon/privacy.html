Privacy Policy

Last updated: November, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how co-pon ("we", "our", or "us") handles your information when you use our Android application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing a transparent experience.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to provide core app functionality:

  • Account Information

    When you sign in using Google Sign-In, we collect your primary email address and basic profile identifier.

  • User Content

    Coupons, wallet items, images, and metadata you explicitly upload or save inside the app.

  • Shared Access Data

    Email addresses of partners you explicitly invite to access or edit shared wallet items.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Authenticate your account and manage your access permissions.
  • Store, display, and synchronize your coupons and wallet items across your authorized devices.
  • Allow authorized partners (using their Gmail addresses) to collaborate on shared wallet items based on your settings.
  • We do NOT sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase services (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Firebase Storage). Access to your stored items is restricted using strict server-side rules to ensure only you and your designated partners can view or modify your content.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You can delete individual coupons or wallet items within the app at any time, which permanently removes them from our servers. If you wish to delete your entire account and associated data, you can contact us.

6. Third-Party Services

Our app uses Firebase services provided by Google LLC for authentication, data storage, and media hosting. These services operate under Google's Privacy Policy and security standards.

7. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to anyone under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
